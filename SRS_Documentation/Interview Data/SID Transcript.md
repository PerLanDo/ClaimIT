# Interview Transcript: Campus Lost and Found Tracker Capstone

Subject: Interview with Security/Investigation Department (SID)

Topic: Current Lost and Found Procedures and Requirements for a Digital Tracker

# 

---

### Part 1: Understanding the Current Process

**Objective:** To understand the current manual workflow, identify pain points, and determine desired features. 

**Q1: Could you walk me through the step-by-step process that occurs from the moment someone turns in a found item to the moment it's claimed or disposed of?**

**SID:** If someone finds an item and turns it over, we record it. We get the name of the finder, the item, and if it is a wallet, we list the things inside. We also record the location where it was found and the time.

For claiming the item, we first ask them about the item. For example, if it is a wallet, we ask for the color and the things inside, and when and where it was lost. If their answers match, we ask for their ID. We record it in the logbook, get their phone number, and ask for their signature. We also record the date and time the item was claimed.

For storing the item, we have a storage area at the back. We have a cabinet for all the items. We sort them and put tape on each item with the location where it was found and the date it was found.

When someone claims an item, for example a tumbler, we ask for the color, the brand, and where and when it was lost. If it matches, we turn it over to them. Then we ask for their information, like ID, cellphone number, the date and time it was claimed, and their signature.

The usual items we receive are umbrellas, tumblers, cellphones, and wallets.

**Q4: When a student comes to inquire about a lost item, what is the typical procedure for searching for it? How long does this usually take per inquiry?**

**SID:**  If the claimant or the finder of the item is already here in the office, the maximum processing time is about 2 minutes, because if they want to claim it, it already has a record.

**Q5: How do you handle communication between different shifts regarding found items or pending claims?**

**SID:**  My duty here is from 7am to 4pm only, and there are also staff assigned from 12pm to 9pm. For the incoming personnel, we turn over the logbook and tell them the items that were turned over during my schedule. That way, if there are claimants, they are aware of the items received today. For previous items, it is not a problem because they are already recorded.

For the schedule when students can claim or turn over an item, we accommodate from 7am to 9pm, Monday to Friday only.

### Part 2: Identifying Pain Points & Challenges

**Q6: What are the biggest challenges you face with the current handwritten logbook system? Have there been issues with legibility or finding information quickly?**

**SID:**  We record the details of the lost and found items in our logbook and also on our computer. So far, we haven’t encountered any challenges in recording them, since it is easy to do.

**Q7: Can you describe a situation where the lack of a photo for a found item made the verification process difficult?**

**SID:** If the claimant doesn’t have a picture as proof of the item, we will interview them. For example, if it is a wallet, we ask about the things inside. If their answers don’t match, we won’t give the item to them.

**Q8: Do you think it is ideal to post an image when reporting a found item to our Lost and Found Tracker Application?**

**SID:** Regarding uploading a picture of the lost and found item in your app, it is okay to take a photo of the item itself. However, for a wallet, we should not take a picture of the contents because it is sensitive information. Sometimes people try to cheat, and we need to keep it safe, especially if there is cash inside. So we only take a picture of the wallet itself, and the contents are only asked about during the claim interview.

**Q9: On an average day, how much of your team's time is dedicated to managing lost and found inquiries and paperwork? How does this change during peak times like finals week or during school events?**

**SID:** During big events in the gym, symposiums, concerts, or orientations, many people come here who have lost their belongings, and some also turn over items. During those times, we spend more of our time handling lost and found matters. Sometimes, we feel that most of our time is spent on this, and we are not able to do anything else. On normal days, only a few people come. For example, now it is already 10am, and we have only received one item.

**Q10: What issues do you encounter with the current item verification process? How do you confirm that an item truly belongs to the person claiming it?**

**SID:** Yes, there are cases of people being dishonest when claiming items. Some are students. Sometimes they say they lost an item, but when we ask questions, their answers don’t match the item. There are those who try to claim items that are not theirs.

To prevent giving an item to someone who is not the real owner, we ask carefully and explain to them, “Sorry, this item is not yours.” Sometimes, they just accept it and say, “Okay, sir.”

**Q11: Do you have a standard policy for how long unclaimed items are kept? What is the process for their disposal, and are there any challenges associated with it?**

**SID:** Yes, we have a policy for it. For example, last year, unclaimed items remain unclaimed for one year. Then we extend for two weeks and display the items in front of our office in the hallway during that time so the owners can claim them. If, after the two-week extension, they are still unclaimed, we put them in a box. Some items even include cash, for example, an amount of 18,000 pesos. We then give these items to KTTO (**Knowledge and Technology Transfer Office**), so in case of calamities, we can donate them to the victims, for example, during a flood.

### Part 3: Desired Features & Ideal Solution

**Q12: If we were to build the perfect digital system for you, what are the top three most important features it would have?**

**SID:** I don’t think I have any features to suggest. If we make it more complicated, it will be hard to use.

**Q13: Let's say in our Lost and Found Tracker Application, we found a lost item. Is it possible that we (the students/finders) keep the found item ourselves and just post it on the Lost and Found Tracker Application? Is that okay?**

**SID:** For us, we ask students that if they find anything, they should turn it over to us. Of course, we can’t force them, because there is no existing policy that imposes a penalty if they don’t turn it over. It depends on them.

If they just keep the item and post it in the app, it won’t be a problem for us. It is even okay if they allow the finder to handle the claim; this could reduce our workload. If any issues arise, such as the item being lost or damaged while in their care, then the two parties should resolve it. We have no liability for that, and it is outside our scope since there are no existing rules for it.

**Q14: Imagine a system that could automatically notify a student if a found item matches the description of something they reported lost. How would that impact your team's workload?**

**SID:** It will be a big help. For example, if the user is notified that their item matches, they will be able to come to our office to claim it.

This app will also help because sometimes students are shy to come to the office, and other students don’t know where to claim or inquire about lost items. That’s why, before, there were many unclaimed items—people were simply not aware.

**Q16: Is it okay to insert/display the phone number/ID number of the user in our Lost and Found Tracker Application?**

**SID:**  Maybe we should not include that. It’s not really necessary, because our number should only be known to people we know. Sometimes we won’t even accept calls from unknown callers, as you know how it is.



**ADDITIONAL INFO:**
Based on last year’s data, there were more unclaimed items. Out of 100 items, only 20 to 30 percent were claimed. But now that students know where to claim their items, the number of unclaimed items has decreased.
